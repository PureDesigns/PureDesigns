Joy of the Lord

By Rachel Wood

October 26, 2025



Where does the joy of the Lord come from?


“But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Against such there is no law. And those who are Christ’s have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires. If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.”
Galatians 5:22-25


Joy is a fruit of the Spirit of Christ. We have joy in the Lord when we draw near to the Lord. When we come to Him by our own desires to know Him, He will bless us by being near to us. This is the key of having joy as our strength. If we go through the motions of the Christian life without actually getting on a heart-to-heart level with Jesus, then we gain nothing and our time is wasted.


We can draw near to Christ by taking time to get on our knees and cry out to Him. Talk to your Father in Heaven. A relationship is only good if both parties are communicating. After you cry out to the Lord, either telling Him your worries and anxieties or just praising Him for who He is and what He has done, get in the Bible to hear from Him. Let the Lord lead you with where to read, and seek His answer to questions with patience and trust that He will answer in His own timing. The most important thing when reading the Bible is to seek to know Jesus better.


God is a wonderful God, Who cares for us despite our sins. If Jesus died for us while we were still sinners (Romans 5:8), and not because of our good merits, then don’t let Satan tell you the lie of “you’re not good enough to come back to the Lord” after you have failed once again.


Do not focus on keeping the joy of the Lord, because feelings are futile. Feelings come and go as the wind. What remains is a relationship with Jesus Christ because He is the One that gives us hope for the future (Titus 3:3-7). Let your hope dwell upon the fact that Jesus has made you into a new creation (2 Cor. 5:17), one that looks like Him (1 John 3:2) so that when you finally see Him face-to-face you will truly know Him.


How wonderful it will be for those who know Him on a personal level at this present time, clinging to Him in the good and bad, when they get home and be with Him for all eternity. It truly will be Paradise!


“In Your presence is fullness of joy.” Psalm 16:11
